Our Story

From a Single
Stitch to an Atelier

Thread & Form Atelier began in 2012 in a small studio in Chennai with one tailoring bench, two sewing machines, and an unwavering belief: that every person deserves clothing that truly fits.

Founded by master tailor Kavitha Rajan, the atelier grew from a neighbourhood word-of-mouth business into a respected tailoring studio serving clients across Tamil Nadu and beyond. Today, our team of six skilled tailors crafts hundreds of garments each month — without ever compromising on quality or care.

We are not a factory. We are an atelier — where every garment is a conversation, and every stitch is a commitment.

Our Belief

Our Tailoring
Philosophy

Four principles that guide every decision we make — from first consultation to final delivery.

01

Fit First

Fit comes first. A garment is never finished until it feels balanced, comfortable and shaped precisely for you.

02

Craft Over Speed

We never rush the work. Every stitch is placed with purpose so your garment remains beautifully made and dependable for years to come.

03

Honest Communication

We give clear, honest advice at every stage because your confidence, satisfaction and lasting trust define our reputation.

04

Sustainability

We favour timeless design over passing trends, creating well-made garments intended to be worn for years.

Our Team

Meet Our
Master Tailors

Each of our tailors brings decades of dedicated experience and a personal speciality. Together, we cover every garment type from casual to couture.

Founder & Lead Tailor

Kavitha Rajan

With 22 years of experience in women's and bridal tailoring, Kavitha founded Thread & Form on the belief that precision and patience are non-negotiable. She specialises in silk, couture occasion wear, and intricate embroidery.

Head of Men's Tailoring

Suresh Kumar

Suresh brings 18 years of structured tailoring expertise, trained in Chennai and refined in Bengaluru's formal wear ateliers. He leads all men's suiting, blazer, and formalwear construction with a rigorous eye for proportion, cut, and finish.

Fabric & Alteration Specialist

Meera Sundaram

Meera's specialty lies in fabric consultation and alteration excellence. With a trained eye for structure and proportion, she transforms existing garments with surgical precision and remarkable sensitivity to original construction.
